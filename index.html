OUR CHURCH

Welcome to Salvation Church Of The Revival Of Faith, where the Spirit of God moves with power, purpose, and passion. We are believers driven to preach the Gospel, revive the broken, deliver the bound, and bring the lost into the saving grace of Jesus Christ.

Through the power of the Holy Spirit, we see chains broken, lives transformed, and destinies restored.
So whether you're seeking healing, restoration, or a deeper relationship with God — there is a place for you here.
Welcome to Salvation Church Of The Revival Of Faith — where souls are saved, lives are changed, and Jesus is Lord.
